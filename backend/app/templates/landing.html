Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

Sign up in under a minute and every feature is unlocked for 14 days — no card required. When the trial ends you can subscribe for £{{ price_gbp }}/month or take a break.

Do I need a website to use WaveBook?

No. Every business gets a free booking website at yourname.wab.is that you can share on Instagram, WhatsApp or Google. You can also link it from your existing site.

How do my customers pay?

Customers pay securely by card for deposits, full payments and online shop orders. You can also accept cash or bank transfer in person — WaveBook tracks everything.

Can my customers reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Every confirmation email includes a booking reference, and customers can reschedule or cancel online using their email and reference — no phone calls needed.

How do reminders work?

Customers get confirmation and reminder emails automatically, and you can send SMS or WhatsApp texts from the app. Reminders are sent up to 24 hours before each appointment.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

No contracts, no setup fees, no hidden costs. One flat £{{ price_gbp }}/month after your trial, and you can cancel anytime.
