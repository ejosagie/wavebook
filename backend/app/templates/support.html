Common questions

How do I start?

Create an account at getwavebook.com/signup, then download the WaveBook app and log in with the same email and password.

How does the free trial work?

New accounts get a free trial with every feature included. When it ends, choose a plan on the subscribe page.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Manage your plan on getwavebook.com/subscribe. Email us if you need help with a payment.

I need to change or cancel my booking.

Use Manage a Booking with the email address and reference from your confirmation email, or contact the business directly.

I forgot my password.

Email us from the address on your account and we'll help you regain access.
